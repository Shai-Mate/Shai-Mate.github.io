Christmas countdown printable: a free paper chain, a 2026 countdown chart and how many links you need

Short answer

A Christmas countdown printable is a paper chain or chart that shows how many sleeps are left until Christmas, with one link or box removed each day. Start on December 1 for 24 links, or on Sunday, November 29 (the first Sunday of Advent in 2026) for 26. Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday.

How to make a paper chain Christmas countdown

  1. Pick a start date. Count the sleeps from that date to Christmas Day; that is how many links you need.
  2. Print and cut the strips. Card stock or heavy paper holds its shape; plain printer paper is fine if you tape it.
  3. Write one small thing on each strip. A job, a kindness, a joke or a tiny tradition, written on the back before you loop it.
  4. Link from the highest number down. Loop the 1 first and hang the chain with the biggest number at the bottom, so the chain gets shorter from the bottom up.
  5. Tear one off each morning. Read the strip out loud and do it that day. Keep the torn links in a jar as a keepsake.

How many links you need, by start date

Count sleeps, not days: a chain started on the morning of December 1 has 24 links and the last one comes off on Christmas Eve. Here is the 2026 count for the four most common start dates.

What to write on each link

A countdown is more fun when every link does something, but it should never turn into a second to-do list. Keep weekday links to five minutes and save bigger plans for the weekends, which in December 2026 fall on Dec 5, Dec 6, Dec 12, Dec 13, Dec 19, Dec 20. Making it work at every age

Toddlers: start a week out and keep the chain short and low enough to reach. The chart on page 2 works as a colouring page, one box a night. School-age kids: let them read the strip aloud and do the tearing. Number the links in big type so they can see the count drop. Tweens: hand them the writing. Many prefer making the chain for younger siblings to tearing it themselves.

Plan around December 2026

Christmas Eve is a Thursday and Christmas Day a Friday this year, so the last full week before Christmas is busy. Put the quickest links on Monday to Thursday that week, and make the Christmas Eve link something calm, such as laying out pyjamas or reading a story by the tree. If you miss a day, tear off two links the next morning; nobody minds.

Questions people ask

When should a Christmas countdown start?

Most start on December 1 and run 24 days to Christmas Eve. In 2026 the first Sunday of Advent is Sunday, November 29, so a chain started then needs 26 links. For toddlers, a one-week chain starting Friday, December 18 is easier to follow.

How many links do I need in a paper chain countdown?

One for every sleep until Christmas: 24 if you start on December 1, 26 from the first Sunday of Advent in 2026, 12 from December 13 and 7 from December 18.

What do you write on a Christmas countdown chain?

Something small to do that day: a little job, a kindness, a tiny tradition such as hot chocolate after school, or a silly challenge. Keep big outings for weekends.

How do I make a countdown work for two kids?

Make one chain each, or share one chain and take turns tearing off links on odd and even days. Two chains avoid the morning race.
