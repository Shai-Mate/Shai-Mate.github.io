White elephant rules, step by step

  1. Everyone brings one wrapped gift that fits the agreed budget, with no name on it.
  2. Put the gifts in one pile and have each player draw a number. Number 1 goes first.
  3. Player 1 opens any gift and shows it to the room.
  4. Each next player chooses: open a new gift from the pile, or steal any opened gift.
  5. A player who loses a gift chooses again right away: open a new one or steal a different gift. You can't take back the gift that was just taken from you.
  6. After a gift's third steal it is frozen and stays with whoever holds it.
  7. When the last gift is opened, player 1 may make one final swap with any gift that isn't frozen. Then the game is over.

Read these out loud once before you start. A minute of reading saves ten minutes of debate.

How many steals in white elephant?

The common answer is three: once a gift has been stolen three times, it is frozen and stays with its third new owner. Count steals per gift, not per person. A tick next to the gift's name each time it changes hands (the steal tally on the free sheet) keeps it clear. Two other limits keep the game moving: a gift can only be stolen once per turn, and you can't immediately take back the gift that was just taken from you.

How long does white elephant take?

Turns that end in a steal chain take longer, so plan on about one and a half to two minutes per player. That's our planning estimate, not a rule:

Group sizeBrisk groupLots of stealing
8 playersabout 12 minabout 16 min
12 playersabout 18 minabout 24 min
16 playersabout 24 minabout 32 min
20 playersabout 30 minabout 40 min
30 playersabout 45 minabout 60 min

For more than 20 people, add a one-minute turn timer, cap steal chains at three, or split into two swaps so nobody waits half an hour for a turn.

White elephant at the office

  • Keep it optional and keep gifts work-safe: no gag gifts that could embarrass someone.
  • Run it at a fixed time slot, for example the last 45 minutes of the party, and say so on the invite.
  • Remote teammates can join if gifts are shipped ahead and opened on camera; the stealing works the same way.
  • Number cards drawn from a bowl are fairer than going around the room.
