Handy facts about 2027

  • January 1, 2027 is a Friday, and December 31, 2027 is also a Friday.
  • 2027 is not a leap year, so February has 28 days.
  • With a Monday-start weekly planner, the first full week of 2027 begins Monday, January 4.

How to print a planner that looks good

  1. Pick your paper size (US Letter or A4) and print at 100% or "actual size" so margins aren't clipped.
  2. Use slightly heavier paper if your printer allows it. Ink shows through less.
  3. Print double-sided for a thinner binder, or single-sided if you like to use the backs for notes.
  4. Punch and bind: a three-ring binder, disc binding or a simple clip all work.
  5. Print a month at a time if you want to save ink. The file isn't going anywhere.
