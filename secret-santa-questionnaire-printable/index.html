How to run a secret santa, step by step

  1. Pick one organizer. One person collects names, sets the rules and runs the draw. If they're also playing, let everyone draw slips themselves.
  2. Set the date and work backwards. Send the invite three to four weeks out. Forms back within a week, names drawn right after, then at least two weeks to shop (more if gifts ship).
  3. Agree on a budget range, not just a cap. "$20 to $30" beats "under $30": nobody opens a $5 gift next to a $30 one. Say whether tax and shipping count.
  4. Collect questionnaires before the draw, so every Santa gets their person's answers with the name.
  5. Draw names fairly. Simplest: names on slips in a bowl, and if you pull your own, draw again. If some people shouldn't be paired (couples, roommates, last year's pairs), the organizer does a private chain draw: shuffle the names into a list, each person gives to the next name, and the last gives to the first. Reshuffle if an excluded pair lands side by side. Nobody gets themselves and everyone gives exactly one gift.
  6. Exchange. Open gifts together at a set time, or deliver anonymously (tips below).

Office and remote variations

  • Office: keep it opt-in and the range modest, make sizes optional, and give people a way to say "no food" or "nothing scented". Set a labelled drop-off box and an exchange time.
  • Remote or spread-out groups: only the organizer collects mailing addresses, the shopping deadline moves earlier for postage, and everyone opens gifts on a video call.
  • Families with kids: run a separate kids' draw with its own budget.

18 secret santa questionnaire questions

Use ten for a quick office form, or all of them for a close group.

The basics

  1. What name should go on the gift tag?
  2. What are your favourite colours?
  3. Favourite snacks or treats?
  4. Hot drink of choice?

What you're into

  1. What hobbies are you into lately?
  2. Do you collect anything?
  3. Favourite shops or places to browse?
  4. Favourite kind of book, music or show?
  5. Cozy night in or day outdoors?

Practical

  1. Sizes (top, socks, gloves), if you're happy to share.
  2. What everyday thing would you love a nicer version of?
  3. Anything you keep meaning to buy?

Good to know

  1. Any allergies or sensitivities (foods, scents, materials)?
  2. Please no: what do you have plenty of?
  3. Any foods or drinks you avoid?

Gift style

  1. Practical, cozy, fun, handmade, an experience or treats?
  2. One nicer thing or a few small ones?
  3. Three ideas within budget, with a shop or link.

Tips that keep it fair and fun

  • Put the budget on every form so it's in front of people when they shop.
  • Two deadlines in the invite: the date forms are due and the date gifts must be ready.
  • No-repeat rule: keep last year's pairs in the organizer's notes and exclude them from this year's draw.
  • Anonymous delivery: number each gift instead of naming it and let the organizer keep the key, or write "From: your Secret Santa" on the gift tag.
  • Keep a spare gift in case someone drops out late.
