Download the free PDF (US Letter)

What to write down for each vaccine

  • Vaccine name (for example rabies, DHPP, Bordetella for dogs; FVRCP or FeLV for cats)
  • Date given and next due date, the two things people ask about most
  • Product and lot number, copied from the certificate or invoice
  • Clinic that gave it, and whether the signed certificate is on file

The rest of a good pet health record

  1. Identity: breed, birth date, microchip number, licence tag and rabies tag (they're different tags)
  2. Vet contacts: regular clinic, emergency clinic, and the portal or email for records requests
  3. Parasite prevention: flea, tick and heartworm doses and dates
  4. Medications and allergies: including any past vaccine reactions
  5. Vet visits: date, reason, weight and what the vet said

Is a printable record "official"?

No. Your own sheet is a summary that helps you answer questions fast. Kennels, travel and licensing usually need the certificate your veterinarian signs, so note where that certificate lives (a drawer, the clinic's portal, an email).
