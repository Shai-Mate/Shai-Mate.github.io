How to teach a child to write a thank you note: the 4-line formula

  1. Dear ___, using the name they call the person (Nana, Uncle Sam, Ms. Lee).
  2. Thank you for the ___. Name the actual gift, not "the present".
  3. One true detail. How they used it, where they'll wear it, or what they love about it. This is the line people remember.
  4. Love / From / Hugs, ___. Kids sign their own name, even if a grown-up wrote the rest.

What to write in a thank you note for a Christmas gift: sample lines

Original examples to read aloud and adapt.

GiftSample line
A toy or gameThank you for the marble run. I built a tower taller than my cat and I'm going to try a loop next.
ClothesThank you for the red mittens. I wore them sledding and my hands stayed warm the whole time.
A bookThank you for the book about sharks. I already read three chapters under my blanket with a flashlight.
Money or a gift cardThank you for the Christmas money. I'm saving half and using the rest for a new skateboard helmet.
A gift they didn't loveThank you for thinking of me this Christmas. It was so nice to open a present from you.

For a gift that missed the mark, thank the person for thinking of them and for the time they spent together. Honest and kind beats a made-up compliment.

A calm thank-you plan for after Christmas 2026

Christmas Day 2026 falls on a Friday and Boxing Day on Saturday, December 26, 2026. A plan that finishes within two weeks:

DayStep
Sat Dec 26Write the gift list while you still remember who gave what (snap photos of tags as gifts are opened).
Sun Dec 27First sitting: 3 notes, for the people who will notice soonest, usually grandparents.
Tue Dec 29Second sitting: 3 more notes. Address envelopes while the child decorates.
Fri Jan 1New Year's Day: finish the family and out-of-town notes, then mail or hand-deliver the first batch.
Fri Jan 8Two weeks after Christmas: last notes out, including teachers and coaches before the first school week ends.
