The Christmas 2027 calendar problem

Christmas Day is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday in 2027, and New Year's Day 2028 is a Saturday too. When a holiday falls on a day you don't work, the day you get instead depends on your province and your employer. For federally regulated workplaces, the rule is a paid day off on the work day immediately before or after, which usually means Monday, December 27 and Tuesday, December 28. Write the actual days into your calendar once your workplace confirms them.

How to set up a 2027 calendar

  1. Pick Sunday or Monday start. Match the calendar on your fridge or phone so dates line up when you copy them across.
  2. Mark the holidays that apply to you. Use the federal column if you work for a bank, airline or other federally regulated employer, otherwise your province's list.
  3. Circle the long weekends. Then book anything that sells out, like campsites and ferries, as early as you can.
  4. Add school breaks and birthdays. Winter, spring and summer breaks come from your school district, so add them once your district publishes them.
  5. Put the bridge days in your vacation request. Ask early in the year, before co-workers claim the same Fridays.

Questions people ask

Is Boxing Day 2027 a stat holiday?

Boxing Day is a federal general holiday and an Ontario public holiday, but not a statutory holiday in B.C. In 2027 it falls on a Sunday.

When is Family Day 2027?

Family Day is Monday, February 15 in both B.C. and Ontario (the third Monday in February). It is not a federal general holiday.

How many stat holidays are there in 2027 in Canada?

Federally regulated workplaces have 10 general holidays. B.C. has 11 statutory holidays and Ontario has 9 public holidays. Other provinces have their own lists.

What day does Christmas fall on in 2027?

Christmas Day 2027 is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday. Under the Canada Labour Code, federally regulated employees get a paid day off on the work day immediately before or after, so many people are off Monday, December 27 and Tuesday, December 28.
