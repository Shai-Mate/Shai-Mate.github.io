Printable Christmas Planner Pages

The holidays go smoother when the moving parts live on paper instead of in your head: who you're buying for, what's been ordered, which evenings are booked, who's covering care for a parent, and what the pet sitter needs to know. Below is a simple week-by-week plan you can follow with any notebook, plus the printable pages we make for each step.

A week-by-week holiday plan

WhenWhat to do
Late OctoberPick one total budget for the season and split it into categories (gifts, food, cards, travel, a buffer).
Early NovemberList everyone you're buying for with a per-person limit. Start ordering anything that ships.
Mid NovemberPut events, visits and travel on one page. If you help care for someone, agree on who covers which days.
Late NovemberPrint the kids' advent calendar so it's ready for December 1.
Early DecemberWrap as gifts arrive, tag them, and tick them off the tracker. Send cards.
Mid DecemberPet-proof the house and leave the sitter sheet on the fridge if you're travelling.
Late DecemberTotal up what you spent, note what to change next year, and set up your 2027 planner.

Tips that actually save time

  • One list, not five. Keep gifts, orders and cards together so you only check one place.
  • Track "wrapped," not just "bought." It's the step people forget until December 23.
  • Write down order numbers. When a parcel goes missing, you'll want the order date and tracking number in one spot.
  • Share the plan. A printed page on the fridge means everyone in the house can see what's done.
