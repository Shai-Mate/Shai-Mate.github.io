Christmas gifts for service providers: who to thank, how much to give and a free 2026 gift and tip list

Short answer

Who to thank, and what's usual

Holiday tips are optional. This is what Canadian etiquette guides commonly suggest; start with the people you see most and stop when you reach your budget.

WhoCommon thank-youCash or gift
Regular babysitterAbout one evening's pay, plus something small from the kidsCash or a gift card
House cleanerAbout the cost of one visit (up to a week's pay if they come weekly)Cash in a card, a gift, or both
Hairdresser or barberUp to the cost of one visit, or double your usual tipEither

The pattern: pay per visit, think one visit; in your home for hours a week, think one evening or one week; not paid by you, think a small gift and a card. Schools, daycares and some employers have gift rules, so ask first. A hairdresser's holiday tip goes on top of the usual tip, and a team of cleaners can share one visit's worth.

When to give in 2026

Earlier is better: it arrives while they are doing their own shopping. Christmas Day is a Friday this year, so aim for Tuesday, December 1 to Monday, December 14 for weekly helpers, and the week of December 14-18 for school, daycare and bus thank-yous, before winter break starts in many districts. For a monthly hairdresser, use the December appointment.

What to write on the tag or card

One specific line beats a long message. Six lines to copy or change:

  1. Thank you for taking such good care of our kids this year.
  2. Coming home to a clean house is the best part of our week. Thank you for everything you do.
  3. Thanks for a whole year of great hair and better conversation. Merry Christmas!
  4. Thank you for the songs, the crafts and the patience. Our little one talks about you every day.
  5. Thank you for getting us there safely every morning, rain or snow. Happy holidays!
  6. Thanks for being such a great teammate this year. Enjoy the break!

Questions people ask

Who should you tip at Christmas?

People who help you regularly: a babysitter, house cleaner, hairdresser, dog walker and daycare educators. For people you don't pay directly, like a bus driver or letter carrier, a small gift and a card.

How much do you give a hairdresser at Christmas?

Up to the cost of one visit, or about double your usual tip, on top of the regular tip at your December appointment.

How much should I give my babysitter for Christmas?

For a regular sitter, about one evening's pay, often with something the kids made. For an occasional sitter, a card and a small gift.

What is a good daycare teacher Christmas gift amount?

Roughly $10 to $40 per educator, or a group gift from several families. Some centres don't allow cash or gift cards, so check first.
