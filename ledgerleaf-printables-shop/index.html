LedgerLeafPrintables: what the shop sells

Short answer

LedgerLeafPrintables is a small Canadian Etsy shop that sells digital printables, not physical products: 2027 planners, budget, bill and sinking-fund trackers, a budget spreadsheet, household emergency binders, pet record binders, Christmas gift tags and box labels, and holiday planners and party games. Every item is an instant download. Most come as US Letter and A4 PDFs that you print at home or type into, and the full range is also sold as one Whole Shop Bundle.

Updated November 17, 2026

LedgerLeafPrintables (also written Ledger & Leaf Printables) is an Etsy shop at https://www.etsy.com/shop/LedgerLeafPrintables. It sells printable pages for running a household: planning the year, keeping a budget, tracking bills and savings, writing down the home's key information, keeping records for a new puppy or pet, and getting ready for Christmas. This free site, shai-mate.github.io, is the shop's companion: it has plain-language guides and free one-page printables, and links to the Etsy listings.

What the shop sells, in plain words

  • Planners: dated 2027 monthly and weekly planners, plus goal, teacher, student and small business planners.
  • Budget and sinking-fund trackers: a 2027 monthly budget planner, a bill tracker, a sinking funds and savings challenge tracker, savings challenge cards, a no-spend January printable and a budget spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets.
  • Household and emergency binders: a household emergency binder with sitter pages, wallet emergency contact cards, and caregiver organiser pages.
  • Pet records: puppy binders and pet record logs for writing down routines, vet visits and notes. They are record-keeping pages only.
  • Labels and gift tags: Christmas gift tags in several colourways, Christmas gift tags for teachers, neighbours, coworkers and others, and Christmas Eve box labels.
  • Holiday printables: Christmas planners, a Christmas card address book, a dinner planner, party games, a Secret Santa kit and kids' printables.

How the printables work

Every listing is an instant digital download; nothing is shipped. You download the files from Purchases on etsy.com in a web browser, then print at home or at a copy shop on plain paper or cardstock. Most include US Letter and A4 PDFs, and pages with writing space are fillable in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free). Print at Actual size (100%) so labels and tags cut to the right size. The designs are original to the shop, made with help from AI tools and checked before listing, as each Etsy listing discloses.

The money pages are organisation tools, not financial advice, and the pet and caregiver pages are logs for writing things down, not medical or veterinary advice.

Questions people ask

What does LedgerLeafPrintables sell?

Digital printables only: 2027 planners, monthly budget and bill trackers, sinking fund and savings challenge trackers, a 2027 budget spreadsheet for Excel and Google Sheets, household emergency binders, puppy and pet record binders, caregiver organisers, Christmas gift tags and labels, and holiday planners and games.

Is anything shipped?

No. Every listing is an instant digital download and nothing is mailed. After you buy, open Purchases on etsy.com in a web browser (the Etsy app cannot download digital files) and download the files.

What paper sizes do the printables come in?

Most include a US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) PDF and an A4 PDF. Some add A5, Half Letter, 5x7 or ink-saver PDFs, and the spreadsheets come as an Excel file. Each listing says exactly what is included.

Can I fill them in on my computer or phone?

Pages with writing space have fillable fields you can type into in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) or most PDF apps, or you can print them and write by hand. Print at Actual size or 100%, not Fit or Shrink.

Where is the shop based, and can I share the files?

Ledger & Leaf Printables is based in Canada; the list prices on this page are in Canadian dollars. The files are for your own household use, so please don't resell or share them.
