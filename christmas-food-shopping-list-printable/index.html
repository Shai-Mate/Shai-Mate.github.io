How to write a Christmas food shopping list

  1. List every meal, not just Christmas dinner. Christmas Eve, Christmas breakfast, dinner, Boxing Day and snacks for guests. Most forgotten items belong to the meals around the big one.
  2. Write the menu, then the ingredients. Go dish by dish and write every ingredient with its amount.
  3. Check the pantry and freezer. Cross off what you already have and note what is close to its date.
  4. Sort what's left by aisle. One pass through the store, fewer missed items, fewer trips.
  5. Give each item a buy-by date. Shelf-stable now, frozen a week out, fresh two or three days before.

When to buy what: a December 2026 shopping timeline

Christmas Day is a Friday this year and Boxing Day a Saturday, so the busy shopping days are Tuesday and Wednesday of that week. Spreading the shop over three trips keeps the fridge from overflowing and the last-minute trip short.

A Christmas baking plan that fits around the shopping

Baking is where most surprise grocery trips come from, because one recipe uses the last of the butter or eggs. Before the pantry shop, list everything you want to bake, who it's for (home, a cookie exchange, teachers, neighbours) and how many batches. Then add up the butter, flour, sugar and eggs across all the recipes and put the totals on the list.

Put freezer-friendly bakes first. A simple 2026 plan: mix and freeze doughs early in December, bake the first round on Sunday, December 13, the second on Saturday, December 19, and pack gift tins on Wednesday, December 23. Leave Christmas Eve for things that need to be fresh.

Do a pantry and freezer check first

Ten minutes in the cupboard saves money: check spice and baking soda dates, count foil, parchment and freezer bags, and make room in the freezer for bakes and in the fridge for the bird. Anything you already have gets crossed off before you leave the house. If you're keeping a gift and food budget too, the free list on our holiday shopping list page covers the gift side.

Questions people ask

What should be on a Christmas food shopping list?

Everything for each meal from Christmas Eve to Boxing Day, grouped by aisle: produce, meat, dairy, bakery, pantry, baking, frozen, drinks and non-food items such as foil and containers. Add each ingredient with its amount, then cross off what you already have.

When should I do my Christmas food shopping?

Split it into three trips. In 2026, buy shelf-stable and baking items around Saturday, December 12, frozen items around Friday, December 18, and fresh produce and dairy on Tuesday, December 22, with Wednesday, December 23 for top-ups only.

How much food do I need for Christmas dinner for 8?

A starting point: an 8-12 lb turkey, about 4 lb of potatoes, 2 lb of each vegetable side, 16 rolls and 2 pies. Adjust for children, big eaters and how much you want left over.

How do I plan my Christmas baking?

List what you want to bake and who it's for, put freezer-friendly bakes first, give each one a bake day, then add up the butter, flour, sugar and eggs across all recipes before you shop.
