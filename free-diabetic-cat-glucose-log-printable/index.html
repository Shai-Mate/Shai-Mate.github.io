Free Diabetic Cat Glucose Log Printable

If your cat has just been diagnosed with diabetes, your vet will likely ask you to track insulin doses, meals and, if you test at home, blood glucose readings. A simple written log makes those vet check-ins much easier.

What to record each day

  • Insulin: time, units given (as prescribed) and injection site
  • Food: what and how much was eaten, and when
  • Glucose readings: time and value, if your vet asked for home testing
  • Water and litter: more drinking or bigger clumps are worth noting
  • Behaviour: wobbliness, weakness or unusual tiredness. Call your vet about these.
  • Weight: weekly, on the same scale
