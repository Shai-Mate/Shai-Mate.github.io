Bingo is the easiest place to start. Give younger kids simple-word cards and grown-ups their own list, with one caller reading both so everyone plays the same round.

Sample New Year trivia for families

  1. How many minutes are in one day? Answer: 1,440 (24 x 60)
  2. What number does the midnight countdown usually start from? Answer: 10
  3. If today is December 31, how many days until next December 31? Answer: 365, or 366 if a February 29 falls in between
  4. What year comes after 2026? Answer: 2027, and January 1, 2027 is a Friday
  5. What do you call the night before New Year's Day? Answer: New Year's Eve

Keep a tie-breaker ready, such as "guess how many seconds are left in the year at 11 PM" (the answer is 3,600).

A countdown to midnight plan

  • 6 PM: crafts, hats and noisemakers while dinner finishes.
  • 7 PM: year in review pages, one per person.
  • 7:30 to 9 PM: bingo, then trivia.
  • 9 PM: write and seal next year's predictions, then keep them in an envelope to open next December 31.
  • 10 PM onward: dancing, snacks and a story break for anyone still awake.
  • 11:50 PM: everyone together, hats on, count down from 10.
