Christmas hostess gift ideas by type

TypeIdeas
For the host the next morningGood coffee or loose tea, a breakfast loaf, jam, pancake mix in a jar with the recipe on the tag. The table is already full; tomorrow isn't.
Inexpensive, under $20A candle, a pretty dish towel, a small bag of clementines with a bow, a box of nice crackers, a jar of hot cocoa mix.

One rule covers most of it: bring something the host can enjoy later, not something they have to deal with now.

What to write on a hostess gift tag

Original lines to copy or adapt.

  • Thank you for having us.
  • Merry Christmas to a wonderful host.
  • Thanks for the lovely evening.
  • For the morning after: put your feet up.
  • Your table is the warmest place in town.
  • A little something for the cook.
  • Thank you for opening your home (again).
  • Cheers to the best host this season.
  • Thanks for feeding us so well.
  • From one happy guest, with love.

Always add who it's from.

Thank you for hosting Christmas: messages to send afterwards

A tag thanks the host before the party; a message the next day thanks them for it. Mention one specific thing.

  • Thank you for hosting Christmas dinner. Everything was delicious and it felt like home. We're still talking about the dessert.
  • Thanks so much for having us last night. You made it look easy, and we know it wasn't. Next one's at our place.
  • Thank you for a wonderful Christmas Eve. The kids haven't stopped talking about it. We loved every minute.
  • Thank you for hosting the party. It was the best night of the season, and it was great to finally meet everyone.
