The parents' Christmas Eve checklist, by time of day

Most Christmas Eve lists fail because they are one long column. Splitting it into four parts of the day makes it obvious what can wait and what can't: the shop has to be done in the morning, but the stockings can only be filled after bedtime. Sixteen jobs, four per part of the day, is enough for most households.

WhenParents' checklist
MorningPick up the last groceries and anything for Christmas breakfast; Check the batteries list: toys, remotes, cameras; Thaw anything the Christmas Day menu needs; Set the time the kids' evening starts
AfternoonFinish the last wrapping and label every gift; Set the table for Christmas Day, or at least lay out the plates; Prep one dish ahead: a salad dressing, a dessert or the stuffing; Charge phones and cameras for the morning
EveningHand out the Christmas Eve box, if you do one; Pyjamas on, a story, and the plate for Santa out; Lay out Christmas morning clothes for everyone; A short call or message to family you won't see tomorrow
After bedtimeFill the stockings and put the gifts out; Set the coffee and a simple breakfast tray; Tidy one room so the morning starts clear; Candles out, tree lights off, doors locked

Write the time your evening starts at the top of the list. Everything in the evening row works better when there is a clear handover from "getting ready" to "being together".

The kids' Christmas Eve checklist

Children like a list they can tick themselves. Keep it to small traditions they can do on the night, not chores, and let them colour the boxes as they go:

  1. Put on Christmas pyjamas
  2. Open the Christmas Eve box
  3. Hang or check my stocking
  4. Set out a plate for Santa
  5. Leave a carrot for the reindeer
  6. Write a note for Santa
  7. Read a Christmas story
  8. Look at the lights outside
  9. Make a hot chocolate
  10. Pick out my clothes for the morning
  11. Help set the table
  12. Go to bed a little early

Move the big jobs out of Christmas Eve 2026

Christmas Eve is a Thursday this year and Christmas Day a Friday, with Boxing Day on Saturday. That makes the weekend of December 19 and 20 the last full weekend before Christmas, and the best time for three jobs that crowd Christmas Eve: the bulk of the wrapping, the main food shop, and a batteries and chargers check. Store hours on Christmas Eve are often shorter, so check your grocery store's hours a week ahead. Our Christmas dinner planner has a dated countdown for the cooking.

Using the checklist at every age

Toddlers: pick four items from the kids' list and do them in the same order every year; the routine is the tradition. School-age kids: give them the full page and a pencil, and let them be in charge of the plate for Santa and the reindeer carrot. Tweens and teens: hand them two jobs from the parents' list, such as setting the table or the breakfast tray. Most are happy to be on the inside of the surprise for younger siblings.

Keep the filled-in page. The memory box on page 2 turns this year's checklist into a keepsake, and next year you will know exactly which traditions your family actually kept.

Questions people ask

What should be on a Christmas Eve checklist for parents?

Last groceries, the last wrapping and labels, batteries and chargers, the Christmas Day table, one dish made ahead, morning clothes, stockings and gifts after bedtime, breakfast set out and a final walk-through: candles out, lights off and doors locked.

What is on a kids' Christmas Eve checklist?

Small traditions they can tick themselves: pyjamas, the Christmas Eve box, a plate for Santa, a carrot for the reindeer, a story, a look at the lights and laying out clothes for the morning. Twelve items is plenty.

What day is Christmas Eve in 2026?

Christmas Eve 2026 is a Thursday, Christmas Day is a Friday and Boxing Day is a Saturday, so many families get a long weekend straight after.

How do I keep Christmas Eve calm?

Move the big jobs earlier. In 2026 the weekend of December 19-20 is the last one before Christmas: use it for wrapping and the big shop, and keep Christmas Eve for a short list.
