Caregiver Medication Tracker: What to Record

When you help a parent or partner with medications, a clear record protects them and makes handoffs between family members, home-care staff and doctors safer.

1. A complete medication list

Name, strength, dose, how and when it's taken, what it's for, the prescriber, and the pharmacy. Include over-the-counter drugs, vitamins and supplements.

2. A daily dose chart

Initial each dose as it's given. This stops missed or doubled doses when several people share care.

3. Refill dates

Note when each prescription runs out and when it was reordered.

4. Side effects and symptoms

Write down the date, what you noticed and what you did. Track only the vitals the care team has asked for.

5. Appointments and changes

Record every visit, any medication change, and questions for next time.

Bring the list to every appointment and ask the pharmacist to review it once a year.
