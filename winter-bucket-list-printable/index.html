How to make a winter bucket list your family finishes

  1. Everyone adds two ideas. Kids follow through on the ones they chose.
  2. Cap it at 20 to 25. A 100-item list looks great on the fridge and gets abandoned by January.
  3. Mark the cost. Aim for at least half free or low cost so the list doesn't compete with the gift budget.
  4. Keep a weather swap. Pair each outdoor idea with an indoor one, and mix types so a week of freezing rain doesn't stall the list.
  5. Date the top 3. Put your three must-dos on actual weekends now, before December fills up.
  6. Tick it where everyone can see it. The fridge works best.
